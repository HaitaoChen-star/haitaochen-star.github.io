About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Human Factors in the Department of Industrial and Manufacturing Engineering at Penn State. My research focuses on how people perceive, understand, and respond to automated vehicles in mixed traffic. I use driving simulation, behavioral measures, statistical modeling, and naturalistic vehicle-trajectory data to examine trust, situation awareness, takeover behavior, and communication between road users.

Research Interests

  • Driver cognition and behavior in mixed traffic
  • Trust in automation
  • Situation awareness
  • Human-machine interface design
